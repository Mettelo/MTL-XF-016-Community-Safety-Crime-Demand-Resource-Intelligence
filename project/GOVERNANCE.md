# Project Governance

Simulated Mettelo engagement using official public/open data. Participants must not claim a real confidential client relationship.

## Data Governance
Document publisher, provenance, frozen version, glossary, critical fields, owners, lineage, DQ rules, transformations, KPI ownership and limitations.

## Responsible Interpretation
Police-recorded crime is influenced by reporting, recording practices and policing activity. It is not a direct measure of total victimisation or neighbourhood danger, and teams must avoid labelling communities as inherently high risk.

Scores, forecasts or flags are decision support unless explicitly justified. Separate analytical evidence from professional, policy, statutory and executive judgement.

## GitHub Governance
Delivery repositories are created under the Mettelo GitHub organisation. Add team members individually; use issues, branches, commits, PRs and reviews where practical. Never commit secrets or prohibited personal data.
